Date: 2026-09-11
Reviewer: Codex
Verdict: Passed locally

- Pure policy evaluation covers reader, riding, private, every group mode,
  redacted, unsupported, stale, and speak outcomes with fixed precedence.
- Parsed bundles produce one decision per message; non-message parser outcomes
  fail closed without entering the speech pipeline.
- Indonesian formatting announces direct sender or group/sender once and omits
  unavailable metadata rather than fabricating it.
- Sanitization normalizes whitespace and unsafe controls while preserving emoji
  joiners, URLs, punctuation, and ordinary Unicode content.
- The 240-character limit preserves word boundaries when possible, never adds an
  ellipsis, does not split surrogate pairs, and cannot let a long announcement
  erase all message content.
- The 180-second boundary follows ADR-005: content is stale only after the limit
  has been exceeded; future timestamps are treated conservatively as age zero.
