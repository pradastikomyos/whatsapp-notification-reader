# P5-T06 Accessibility And Responsive Review

Date: 2026-09-12
Status: Static/local review passed with physical accessibility review pending the
final device campaign.

## Reviewed And Corrected

- All route content scrolls vertically; the shared navigation row scrolls
  horizontally rather than clipping labels on small screens.
- Material controls retain their minimum targets. Group policy rows are now full
  48dp selectable radio targets rather than radio-button-only targets.
- Switches, test-speech action, and speech-rate slider have explicit TalkBack
  descriptions. Group selection has title-specific descriptions.
- The app follows the system dark theme through Material light/dark color
  schemes. No route relies on fixed light-only foreground/background colors.
- Text is resource-backed and each screen is bounded to a readable max width;
  scrolling covers large font scale layouts.
